DoseWise helps you take the right medication at the right time and shows how you are doing over weeks and months, without asking you to trust a cloud with your health. Everything is encrypted on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no ads.

STAY ON SCHEDULE
- Flexible schedules: fixed times on chosen weekdays, every N days, or as needed.
- A clean daily timeline with a doses-taken-today progress ring.
- Take or Skip a dose right from the notification.
- Snooze a dose when life gets in the way.

UNDERSTAND YOUR ADHERENCE
- Adherence rate over 7 and 30 days, plus your current streak.
- A month heatmap and per-medication breakdowns.
- Export a doctor-visit PDF report to share at appointments.

MADE FOR REAL LIFE
- Refill tracking with low-supply warnings.
- Family profiles for yourself and people you care for.
- Optional reminders per medication.

PEACE OF MIND
- Optional biometric app lock.
- All data is stored in one file encrypted with AES-256-GCM, with a key held in the Android keystore.
- Encrypted backup and restore: a passphrase-protected file you control.

PERMISSIONS, IN PLAIN WORDS
- Notifications and vibration: to remind you when a dose is due.
- Exact alarms: so Android can deliver those reminders at the time you set rather than later. It is used only for dose reminders.
- Boot completed: so your reminders come back after the phone restarts.
- Biometrics: only for the optional app lock.
- DoseWise does not ask for contacts, location, camera or microphone. The store build declares no network permission.
